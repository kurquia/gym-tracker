Changes made for Gym Tracker (tool/build_body_model.py and
tool/export_zanatomy.py): took the bones (without the smallest inner ones),
the muscles and the visible tendons; left out fascia, bursae, sheaths and
other connective tissue; covered the head with its skin (joined into one
surface with the eyeballs, holes filled, smoothed) and left out the skull and
the face, jaw and eye muscles under it; merged the left and right side of
each muscle;
named the three trapezius parts by position; decimated the meshes, rotated
and rescaled them, and stored them in a compact binary format.
